# SOP-ESD-009 Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) Control

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - written for the mini-mes-quality demo. Fictional line, fictional values. Not a real
> procedure and not derived from any company's documents. Do not use for real production.

Revision: A (synthetic) | Applies to: whole electronics area

## 1 Purpose
This procedure keeps ESD-sensitive boards and components from being damaged by static discharge.

## 2 Personal grounding
2.1 Every person entering the ESD area tests their wrist strap and ESD footwear at the tester at the entrance.
2.2 The test must be repeated at the start of every shift and after every break.
2.3 A person whose test fails may not handle boards until the strap or footwear is replaced and a new test
passes.

## 3 Environment
3.1 Relative humidity in the ESD area is kept between 30 and 70 percent.
3.2 ESD flooring and work-surface resistance are checked every month and the results are recorded.

## 4 Packaging and transport
4.1 Boards are moved only in ESD-safe totes or shielding bags.
4.2 Foam or plastic that is not ESD-safe is not allowed within 30 cm of an exposed board.
