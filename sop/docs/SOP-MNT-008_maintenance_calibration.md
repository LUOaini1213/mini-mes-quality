# SOP-MNT-008 Preventive Maintenance and Calibration

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - written for the mini-mes-quality demo. Fictional line, fictional values. Not a real
> procedure and not derived from any company's documents. Do not use for real production.

Revision: C (synthetic) | Applies to: all line equipment

## 1 Purpose
This procedure sets the preventive maintenance (PM) intervals and the requalification needed before equipment
returns to production.

## 2 Intervals
2.1 Printers: stencil-support and squeegee inspection every week; full PM every month.
2.2 Pick-and-place machines: head calibration every two weeks; vacuum nozzles are replaced after 500,000 picks.
2.3 Reflow ovens: conveyor chain lubrication and flux-trap cleaning every month.
2.4 AOI stations: camera and lighting calibration every month with the calibration target.

## 3 Drift monitoring
3.1 Maintenance reviews the placement-offset trend of every pick-and-place machine every week, split by shift.
3.2 A machine whose offset trend rises in one shift only is inspected for temperature-related drift (for example
the night shift, when the hall is cooler).

## 4 Requalification after maintenance
4.1 After a PM or a repair, the equipment is requalified by running 5 boards that must all pass inspection.
4.2 Requalification is recorded in the MES; until then the equipment stays on HOLD.

## 5 Records
5.1 PM records are kept for 3 years.
