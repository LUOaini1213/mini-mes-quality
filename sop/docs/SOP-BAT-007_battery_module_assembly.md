# SOP-BAT-007 Battery Module Assembly and Cell Handling

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT - written for the mini-mes-quality demo. Fictional line, fictional values. Not a real
> procedure and not derived from any company's documents. Do not use for real production.

Revision: B (synthetic) | Applies to: ASM-01 | Route step: 50 ASSY

## 1 Purpose
This procedure covers the assembly of the inspected protection board with lithium-ion cells into a battery
module.

## 2 Cell incoming check
2.1 Every cell's open-circuit voltage is measured before assembly. Cells must be between 3.55 and 3.65 V.
2.2 Cells within one module must match within 10 mV of each other.
2.3 Cells with dents, leaks or damaged wrap are rejected and placed in the metal quarantine bin.

## 3 Handling
3.1 Wear insulated gloves when handling cells and busbars.
3.2 Never place tools on top of a module; use the tool tray.
3.3 Cells are stored at about 30 percent state of charge in the cell store.

## 4 Busbar welding and fastening
4.1 Busbar screws are tightened to 4.0 N m with a tolerance of plus or minus 0.2 N m using the controlled
torque driver.
4.2 Every weld is checked by the pull-test sample plan: 1 module in every 50.

## 5 End-of-line electrical test
5.1 Insulation resistance between the cell stack and the housing must be at least 100 megohm at 500 V DC.
5.2 A module that fails the insulation test may not be reworked on the line; it is sent to the failure-analysis
lab.

## 6 Thermal event response
6.1 If a cell vents or smokes, press the emergency stop, evacuate the cell and call the emergency response
team. Do not try to move a smoking module by hand.
6.2 Place a suspect module in the sand-filled thermal containment box only if it is safe to do so with the
long-handled tongs.
